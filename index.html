About Doug

Your neighbour.
Your accountant.
Your advocate.

For over three decades, Quispamsis has been home. I've raised two children here, built my career here, and watched our community grow into the #1 place to live in Atlantic Canada.

As a CPA, CA with more than 40 years of experience, I've spent my career helping small businesses with income tax and financial planning. For the past 12 years, I've served as Treasurer for the Town of Rothesay, giving me hands-on experience with municipal budgets, infrastructure planning, and the financial decisions that shape a community.

Now I want to bring that same expertise and dedication to Quispamsis Town Council — to ensure your tax dollars are spent wisely, our infrastructure keeps pace with growth, and every resident has a voice.

My Platform

What I Stand For

Practical solutions built on experience, accountability, and a genuine love for our community.

Fiscal Responsibility

With 40+ years as a CPA and 12 years managing Rothesay's finances, I know how to stretch every tax dollar. I'll push for transparent budgeting, smart spending priorities, and keeping our property tax rate stable while maintaining the services you depend on.

Infrastructure & Growth

Our roads, water systems, and recreation facilities need attention. With $12M+ in road capital already budgeted, I'll ensure projects are completed on time and within budget. I support smart, managed growth while protecting our town's character.

Community & Sports

The Quispamsis QPlex is the heart of our sports community. I'll champion continued investment in recreation, youth programs, and facilities that bring families together. A strong community is built on the connections we make.

Housing & Affordability

Our Housing Action Strategy is a start, but we need to fight harder for federal and provincial funding. I'll advocate aggressively for Housing Accelerator Fund support and work to ensure Quispamsis remains affordable for families and seniors alike.

Safety & Livability

From childcare access to long-term care, from road safety to e-scooter regulations, the issues that affect daily life matter. I'll bring a practical, evidence-based approach to bylaw updates and ensuring our town stays safe for everyone.

Transparency & Accountability

You deserve to know where your money goes. I'll push for clearer budget reporting, accessible council updates, and genuine public engagement on the new Municipal Plan and Zoning By-law. Your voice should shape our town's future.
